Bio

Jian Weng is an Assistant Professor at KAUST's Computer Science. His research interests lie on full-stack computing systems' design and implementation, including but not limited to designing and analyzing accelerators, the accelerator-associated software-stacks as well as exploiting the potentials of existing devices. His works have been recognized with one IEEE Micro Top Picks Honorable Mentions, and one MICRO Best Paper Runner-up. Jian Weng earned his Ph.D. in Computer Science from UCLA in 2023, advised by Prof. Tony Nowatzki.
